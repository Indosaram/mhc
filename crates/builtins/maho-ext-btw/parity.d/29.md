Notes:
- `index.ts` is the extension factory: `src/lib.rs` registers the same four lifecycle hooks
  (`session_before_switch`, `session_before_fork`, `session_shutdown`, `input`) and the `/btw`
  command with the pinned usage/error notices and widget/notify surfaces. The `input` hook closes
  only a settled panel; the other three abort.

  match for every status.

  rather than `crypto.randomUUID()`; both are per-query unique discriminators for cache affinity.
