`auto_credentials::is_credential_path` is the auto-preset name check from `auto-credentials.ts`. It is distinct from `external_dir::is_credential_path` (external_dir.rs:84), which guards mhc's own `auth.json` for the SD-1 `credential_file` permission.

The modules are declared in `src/lib.rs` (`always_scope`, `auto_credentials`, `auto_paths`, `auto_policy`, `auto_program_rules`, `auto_shell_grammar`, `auto_shell_segments`, `internal_tools`, `preset_registry`, `dispatch`, `dispatch_metadata`, `dispatch_policy`, `read_permission`).

`preset-registry.ts` keys a WeakMap by the SessionManager object. The Rust port keys by session id and compares readers with `Arc::ptr_eq`, which gives the same "retiring an older load never removes a newer one" rule.

Earlier workers falsely claimed dispatch and read-permission were absent; `src/dispatch.rs`, `src/dispatch_metadata.rs`, `src/dispatch_policy.rs`, and `src/read_permission.rs` are fully ported and integrated with live authorizer registration and lifecycle in `src/lib.rs`, with dedicated tests in `tests/dispatch.rs` (11 tests), `tests/dispatch_metadata.rs` (2 tests), `tests/dispatch_policy.rs` (3 tests), and `tests/read_permission.rs` (5 tests).
