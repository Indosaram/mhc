Notes:
- CommandMonitor's registration, output subscription and settle stay in `MonitorRegistry::register`; the record, line buffer, filter, fire window and runaway guard live in `command_monitor_source.rs`, re-exported from `monitor_registry`.
- `FileMonitor` is the record type (`pub type FileMonitor = FileMonitorRecord`); `register` proves the target and calls `new_file_record`, and `check` runs `check_file_once`.
- Native divergence in the check pass: a target is re-proven through `realpath_without_open` plus the identity-bound checkpoint (`durable_file::file_checkpoint_with_identity`), not through a held handle.
- `onMonitorEnded` now removes the state dir of a watch that ended with reason exit, timeout or killed, and keeps it for `disposed`. `MonitorEndedEvent.monitor_id` carries the stable id that upstream reads through `manager.monitorIdOf`.
