# Forward-ported websearch providers (not pinned parity)

The pinned senpi checkout (`59d58cee`, v2026.10.27-2) carries 26 provider modules under
`builtin/websearch/websearch/providers/`. The eight modules below exist in this crate but have no
counterpart in that tree, so they are **forward ports**, not parity targets: they must never be
counted as pinned-senpi coverage, and no ledger row claims they are.

Provenance was checked rather than assumed. senpi `origin/main` does carry commit
`9f708ec30d` ("feat(websearch): add Firecrawl provider, keyed and keyless"), but
`git merge-base --is-ancestor` reports it is **not** an ancestor of the pin, so it landed after the
pin. Copies of these sources were also found under `@oh-my-pi/pi-coding-agent@18.12.6` in the local
bun cache, which is where the draft sources came from. Either way they are post-pin or
foreign-origin, and neither is senpi at the pin.

They are wired like the pinned providers: `config.rs` registers all 17 new provider ids (these
eight plus the subscription and keyless families), `build_search_request` returns a typed
`SearchBuildError` instead of panicking, and `searxng`/`public` require a `baseUrl`. The pinned
provider set is unchanged by this work.
