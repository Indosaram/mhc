`resolveSpawnContext` starts from `getShellEnv()` (agent `bin` dir prepended to PATH).
maho-tools cannot depend on maho-core (which owns `getBinDir`), so
`BashToolOptions.shell_env` injects the provider; maho-core `sdk.rs` and maho-cli
`task_session.rs` pass `maho_core::utils::shell::get_shell_env` for bash and powershell.
Without a provider the process env is used, as before.
