Notes:

- reserved-keyword-rainbow: `createReservedKeywordHighlight` needs core `reserved-keywords.ts`
  (maho-core lane). The colour spectrum and styler are ported.
- system-theme: the generator, contrast and appearance pieces are ported. Registering the theme in
  the theme registry is not done.
- tips, model-change-notice, tool-execution-animation and remote-delivery-message are ported but
  not yet called from `interactive_mode.rs` (drift-owned).
- stderr/stdout-fd-redirect: senpi loads `dup`/`dup2` through `bun:ffi`. The Rust port uses rustix's
  safe `dup`/`dup2_stdout`/`dup2_stderr`, and its tests run against a stand-in descriptor.
- footer-right-label is n/a: the footer is the intentional v7 HUD redesign (`docs/divergences.md`
  UX-2).
- runtime-notice and its presenter are n/a: they are the "running on Node.js, install Bun" notice,
  and mhc is a native binary. maho-cli parity already marks `bun-runtime.ts` n/a.
- mermaid: the layout engine is the external `grok-mermaid` npm package, which is not in the senpi
  tree.
- message-path-links needs core `message-path-resolution.ts`, and resume-rebind needs core
  `moved-sessions.ts` and `resume-target.ts`. Neither has a Rust port yet.
- session-control-*: handed off to the senpi-interactive lane. The ports are committed on
  branch `sync5152/interactive` at f51044775 (session_control_{attach,commands,endpoint,feed,host,
  lifecycle,registry,rpc_commands,server,session_commands,surface,wake}.rs plus
  tests/session_control_endpoint.rs). The census rows (maho-interactive-24 feed, -26 lifecycle,
  -27 registry, -30 session-commands, -31 wake) belong to this ledger and close when that branch
  merges. The two branches' lib.rs hunks do not overlap.
