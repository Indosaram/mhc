`grok-mermaid` is an npm dependency of the pinned senpi checkout, not one of its source files, so it
carries no row in the crate's source-root audit. The rows above record the deliberate partial port.

Goldens are generated from pinned senpi by `tests/generate-components32.mjs`,
`tests/generate-components32-tools.mjs` and `tests/generate-components32-renderers.mjs`.
`tests/components32_foundation.rs`, `tests/components32_tools.rs` and
`tests/components32_renderers.rs` exercise the borders, continuity notices, markdown transformer
ordering, user/branch/compaction/skill cards, render signatures, source boxes, the word diff, the
diff renderer, visual truncation and all eight built-in tool renderers.

Coverage: 14 deterministic Rust tests across the three files. The renderer corpus compares call and
result renders byte-for-byte against pinned senpi at 80 columns; the shell call body is compared
with ANSI stripped because the crate's todo 31 `highlight_code` classifies `echo` differently from
senpi's highlight.js, which is a `highlight_code` fidelity gap rather than a renderer one.
