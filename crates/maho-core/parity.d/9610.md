Notes:

- `retry-fallback/model-config-error.ts` is partial: the reporter logs `model_config_error` through the fallback
  log only. `AgentSessionEvent` (maho-ext-api) has no `ModelConfigError` variant yet.
- `provider-failure.ts` is done: the agent_end projection freezes `selected_model_of` (fallback chain original, else
  the session model) when the end arrives and records `provider_failure_of(messages, selected)`, read through
  `AgentSession::last_provider_failure`. The maho-ext-api agent_end envelope carries no `failure` field yet (core-ext
  owns maho-ext-api).
- `retry-fallback/usage-limit.ts` (c2bef0ffa4): the scope is read by matching `UsageLimit` only, so a `model_config`
  cause yields no scope.
- `work-obligations.ts` is partial: the queued-carrier join and written-ticket settlement
  (`_queuedTicketCarrier`, `_writtenTicketEntries`) need persist watchers maho does not have; the DifferentItems
  refusal, stored item ids and the QueuedDeliveryCleared reason are ported.
- `lifecycle-stop.ts` / `lifecycle-wait-items.ts` / `lifecycle-ledger.ts` are owned by the core-session lane
  (parity.d/9606); this lane consumes them from agent_session.
- `structured-session-lane.ts` is wired after lane integration: `AgentSession` constructs the lane from
  `InternalAgentChannel`, publishes ordinary events through `emit_event`, exposes `subscribe_structured`
  and disposes the lane. `the_structured_lane_orders_block_controls_among_session_events` drives a
  capture-enabled provider stream and checks start/control/end ordering, provider capture and refusal
  without an internal channel. Runtime validation remains part of the final integrated gate.
