The 40.md blocker was the missing safe `delete process.env.ORCA_PI_PREFILL`. It is closed: `take_process_prefill` latches process-wide and records the delete in `maho_ext_api::process_env`, which every child-environment builder applies. maho-cli's extension_registry binds it.
`crates/maho-process-env` was checked and deliberately not used. Its contract only allows calls while the process is single-threaded, and `session_start` runs inside the multi-threaded runtime.
Ported now: upstream returns before the `delete` when the prefill is unset or empty. The take no longer latches or deletes in that case.
Kept local behaviour (98a00583): a draft typed before startup completes gets the prefill pasted at the cursor instead of being replaced.
