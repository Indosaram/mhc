The effect ledgers of a classic connection live under the session's identity (upstream keys them by the session
object) and are released by the session's dispose hook; a routed session's live under its routing handle, the key its
record stream reads them under. `effectContext().by` is `local` on the classic handler, as upstream answers without a
client info.

Tests ported (src/connection_handler.rs `new_session_tests`): rpc-queued-input-edit.test.ts, both cases of
rpc-queued-input-revision.test.ts, rpc-pending-inputs.test.ts "refuses more than 64 known ids" plus the no-custody
snapshot shape, and get_effect_status on the loaded ledger and an unreadable `sessionPath`. Not ported:
rpc-pending-inputs.test.ts "reads queued input with explicit outcomes and publishes each change" (needs identified
input admitted into the pending-input store; maho-core/parity.d/51529.md names that gap).
