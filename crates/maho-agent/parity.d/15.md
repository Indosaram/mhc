## Notes

- The supplied per-file baseline sums to 169. Inspection of literal `it` declarations and
  every `it.each` table yields 187 executable cases: accept 20, drive-public 30, structural 27,
  terminal 6 and restore 11; other supplied counts stand. Rows record expanded case counts.
  This is a source-table audit, not a claim that the TypeScript suite was executed here.
- The owned files `lane.rs`, `harness.rs`, `drive/structural.rs`, `drive/tools.rs` are partial
  ports with real durable admission, command, queue, abort, usage and replay-checkpoint slices.
  The provider drive dispatcher and its sibling phase modules are absent in this checkout.
  Equal or greater Rust counts above never imply semantic parity; helper/regression tests
  are explicitly distinguished from complete upstream integration scenarios.
- Physical test counts: `harness_drive_lane.rs` 29, `harness_drive_accept.rs` 31,
  `harness_drive_structural.rs` 7, `harness_drive_tools.rs` 9, `harness_drive_retry.rs` 2.
  The suite rows allocate subsets without double-counting. Extra regression/Drive-type
  checks are not assigned to an upstream suite row.
- `harness/tools/**`, `harness/skills.ts`, `harness/utils/segmented-read-view.ts` and
  `harness/utils/read-folders/**` are declared as placeholder modules only.
- `harness/env/nodejs.ts` carries both the filesystem and the shell/execution capability;
  `harness/types.ts` carries the union of the session lane's `FileSystem` contract (owned
  `FileContent`, option structs) and the rest lane's declarations, because both lanes ported
  `types.ts` and the merged crate keeps one object-safe `FileSystem`.
