## Notes

- `dag-lint.ts` and `dag-quiet-nodes.ts` are present as functions in `src/dag_tool.rs`
  (`lint_dag_definition_nodes`, `quiet_nodes_notice`). They were not duplicated into new modules;
  the dag tool is their only consumer upstream as well.
- The Rust `lint_dag_definition_nodes` additionally requires DELIVERABLE:/SCOPE:/VERIFY: markers,
  which upstream states in the same contract sentence. That is a stricter warning set, not a
  missing behavior: warnings never reject upstream and do not reject here.
- Status-edge detection and the terminal observer ledger are one mechanism in Rust: the store write
  listener (`src/component.rs:389`) produces the edge and `src/engine_store_chain.rs:57` observes
  the settled record for spend. Upstream splits them across `store-status-edge.ts` and
  `terminal-observers.ts`; both rows point at the real code.
- The eleven `partial` rows are honest splits, not passes: each note names the exports that are
  absent. The largest remaining gap is the omo-side host plumbing family
  (`shard-routing.ts`, `shard-crash-notice.ts`, `host-execution-mode.ts`,
  `host-prewarm-intent.ts`), whose native primitives live in `maho-rpc` and `senpi-task` but are
  not composed into a task-component host runtime here.
- Two rows in `44.md` are corrected by this fragment: `runtime-context.test.ts` had 1/3 and
  `task-skill-loader.test.ts` had 1/2. The true counts are 6/4 and 9/4, and the uncovered cases are
  named in the reason column rather than relabelled N/A.
