## OpenCode support is OUT OF SCOPE (not a pending choice)

The port target is senpi + oh-my-openagent; OpenCode support is not required. Accordingly this lane
authored **no** OpenCode adapter, OpenCode-only migration, cross-process sidebar, locale, integration
or routing support. Specifically:

- `config-startup/opencode-routing-notice.ts` (`OPENCODE_ROUTING_NOTICE_MIGRATION_ID`,
  `createOpenCodeRoutingNoticePlan`) was **authored earlier in this lane and then REMOVED** when the
  scope was corrected. It is OpenCode routing support: its whole purpose is to read the OpenCode
  edition's own config (`omo-native/bin/lib/setup-opencode-models.js`: `readEditionRouting`,
  `openCodeRoutingGap`, `openCodeRoutingNotice`) and report settings Native ignores. Nothing of it
  remains in `src/index.rs` or `tests/notices.rs`, and it is not registered in `discover`.
- The three upstream `opencode-routing-notice.test.ts` cases that depend on the real gap reader are
  **excluded** with it.
