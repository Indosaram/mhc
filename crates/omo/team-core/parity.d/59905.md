## Notes

- Lock records are 5 lines (`<tag> <uuid>\n<pid>\n<epoch_ms>\n<incarnation>\n<process_start>\n`) using `maho_memory_core::locks::{get_pid_liveness, get_process_start_identity, start_identities_conflict}`.
- `with_lock_lease` passes a `LockLease` whose `hand_over(pid)` writes a 5-line record naming the child PID and its process-start identity, and whose `restore()` runs compare-and-rename under `with_record_guard`.
- `TeamCoreError::LockWaitTimeout` carries `Option<LockHolder>` parsed from the contended lock record, and `TeamCoreError::LockLeaseLost` fires when the lock record changes or its reclaim guard is busy during handover.
