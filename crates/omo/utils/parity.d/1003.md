The assembly source exposes AbortController, AbortSignal and AbortRegistration.
Linux cancellation uses an owned pidfd, independent of the Child reaping path.
Setup failure kills and reaps the owned child; explicit kill remains SIGKILL.
Repeated exited calls use the cached code. spawn_sync ignores the signal.

The Linux cases exercise wait_with_output cancellation after child readiness,
signal-less stdout/stderr preservation and sync spawn with an aborted signal.
The off-Linux cases assert Unsupported for signal-bearing spawn and preserve
signal-less/sync behavior. processes.rs is Unix-gated: these cases do not cover
Windows. Off-Linux cancellation is an OPEN platform gap, not accepted parity.

The six Linux lifecycle regressions and bounded owned-child fixture are applied
in tests/spawn_cancellation.rs. They cover abort, abort during exited, shared
signal, already-aborted signal, cached exit and explicit kill after trapped
SIGTERM. Cancellation success is observed before cleanup can send SIGKILL.
Original runtime-shims cases mapped by todo 2 remain preserved; this fragment
does not replace their mapping or claim the entire runtime suite is covered.
