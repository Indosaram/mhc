Plan Todo 1074 wires parsed CLI extension flags into the loaded extension runtime during single-session startup in `crates/maho-cli/src/cli/runtime.rs`.

## Details
In upstream `main.ts`, command-line flags not claimed by built-in CLI options are validated against the flag registry of loaded extensions and applied to the active extension runtime.

In native `crates/maho-cli/src/cli/runtime.rs`, `parsed.unknown_flags` previously retained `--permission-preset` and `--permission` in `Args`, but never called `extensions.runtime.set_flag(name, flag_value)`. Consequently, `maho-ext-permission-system` received `None` from `runtime.get_flag` on `SessionStart`, defaulting to `FullAccess` (`* = allow`) and bypassing non-interactive trust boundaries.

Filtering `parsed.unknown_flags` against registered extension flag names (`extension.flags`) and transferring matching values via `extensions.runtime.set_flag`:
1. Restores exact 1:1 behavioral parity with upstream `main.ts` extension flag binding semantics.
2. Avoids blanket binding of unregistered options while ensuring legitimate extension flags reach their owning lifecycle handlers.
3. Enables `PermissionSystem` to enforce `--permission-preset workspace` and `--permission <rule>` policies under installed execution.

Regression verification is authentically enforced through parent's installed QA harness (`installed-coding-trust-qa.mjs` permission scenario) with zero synthetic unit test proxies.
