Every runtime (print, rpc, interactive, app-server, multi-session host, `--list-models`) mounts the same `assembled_factories`, which now includes the `claude-sdk-oauth` and `cursor-cli-oauth` lanes in pinned order before `config-reload`/`tool-search`/`mcp`. Both lanes register unconditionally and resolve their executable per turn. `enabledBuiltinExtensions`/`disabledBuiltinExtensions` filter builtin and `codemode` ids; vendored `pi-*` packages are shadowed while their builtin is active; `--no-extensions` drops linked user ports on every path.
