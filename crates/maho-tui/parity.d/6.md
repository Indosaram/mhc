Golden parity (tools/golden, generated from pinned senpi):

- `tui-width-corpus`: senpi `visibleWidth` over 68 corpus strings (ASCII, CJK, combining marks, Indic/Myanmar/Thai clusters, ZWJ families, flags and tag flags, keycaps, skin tones, VS16, ANSI/OSC/APC sequences) plus every scalar value U+0020..U+323AF and U+E0000..U+E01EF; compared by tests/width_golden.rs with 0 mismatches.
- `tui-wrap-cjk-emoji-7`: `wrapTextWithAnsi` (plain and SGR-styled) and `truncateToWidth` of a CJK+emoji string at width 7; compared byte-for-byte by tests/width_golden.rs.

Rust-side notes:

- Word segmentation uses `icu_segmenter` 2.3.0 dictionary mode (ICU rules plus CJK/SE-Asian dictionaries, as ICU4C behind `Intl.Segmenter`); graphemes use `unicode-segmentation` 1.13.3; widths use senpi's own tables (generated into src/unicode_tables.rs).
- native-platform.ts: senpi's `.node` addon becomes a static registry of native helpers; no dynamic loading.
- examples/key-tester.rs ports test/key-tester.ts. It redraws the frame directly on ProcessTerminal because TuiMainScreen belongs to todo 7.
