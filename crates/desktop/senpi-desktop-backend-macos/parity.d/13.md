## Divergences from the upstream bytes

- `ax/actions.rs`: mhc has no `ax/date` or `ax/popup`, so `AxRequests` carries no `read_date` and `write_value` writes the `CFString` only; the date and popup tests are dropped with them.
- `input/holds.rs`, `input/menu_key.rs`, `EmergencyRelease`: absent in mhc; `Request` guards sit in `MacInput::{pointer,type_text_interruptible,key_chord}` and the in-process `release_all` also tells the ledger via `button_up`.
- `capture/{route,sck}` tests: the `route` and `sck` modules do not exist in mhc, so their test-only changes have nothing to attach to.
