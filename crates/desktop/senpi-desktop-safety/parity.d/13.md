- mhc base has no hold signals, emergency release worker, bounded release or durable `with_stop_commit`: `commit_stop` is the authority commit callback plus publication; the `stop.rs`/`status.rs` split, `published`, `stop_commit_lock`, `stop_epoch`, `suspend_input`, `stop_pending`, `published_status`, `complete_stop` are introduced here.
