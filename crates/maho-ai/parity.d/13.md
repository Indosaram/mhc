## Notes

- Rust-only shims with no senpi counterpart (they exist so the OAuth listeners can bind a real socket):
  `src/auth/oauth/loopback.rs` (2 tests) and `src/auth/oauth/transport.rs` (2 tests). `loopback.rs` carries a
  `#[cfg(test)]`-only forced-bind-failure seam mirroring senpi's `__setAnthropicOAuthNodeApisForTests`,
  compiled out of production builds.
- Two parity bugs surfaced while porting the credential-pool cases and were fixed in this lane:
  `PooledCredentialType` serialized `OAuth` as `o_auth` (the stored tag must be `oauth`), and the
  slot-scoped OAuth refresh wrote the refreshed credential back through `to_flat_credential()`, dropping
  `accounts`/`pinned`; senpi writes the whole pooled object back (`to_stored_credential()`).
- The 13-cursor-oauth lane shim in `src/auth/oauth/cursor.rs` was deleted on merge: the module now uses
  `crate::auth::types` and `crate::auth::oauth::pkce::generate_pkce()`.
- `providers/cursor.ts` ports the factory shape only (id/name/baseUrl, `cursor-agent` api); its
  `fetchModels`/`restoreModels` live in `api/cursor-agent.ts` and `cursor/store-migration.ts` (todo 12).
- `providers/register-builtins.ts` re-exports `crate::compat`; `providers/data/.manifest.json` is embedded
  as `src/providers/data/model_data_manifest.json` for `getBuiltinModelDataGeneratedAt()`, and the
  per-provider `data/*.json` catalogs are folded into `crates/maho-ai/data/models.json` (todo 5).

  round-trip tests (its own rows are reported in rows/ai-replay.md and in the todo 10/12 fragments).
